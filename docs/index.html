HAND CONTROL

Your hands move the Hub board.

  1. Choose HANDS in your dashboard to start the camera. It runs on your own device; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. You do: Start the camera explicitly in your Hub.

  2. The fingertip is your cursor. Hold it still over a control and it clicks. You do: Point at a control and hold still.

  3. A quick pinch clicks. Hold it and move to drag a block or scroll a panel; open your fingers to drop it. You do: Pinch, hold and move, then let go.

  4. A fist takes control of the screen. A fist again, a point or a pinch hands it back. You do: Make a fist.

  5. Move the open palm and the board follows. Rest your hand to finish the move. You do: Open your hand and move it.

  6. Push the palm toward the camera to zoom in; pull it back to zoom out. Pan and zoom never run together. You do: Move your hand toward or away from the camera.

  7. In the 3D view, hold the fist and turn your wrist: the board and its panels turn with it. You do: Hold a fist and turn your wrist.
