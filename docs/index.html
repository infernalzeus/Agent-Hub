AUTONOMOUS LLM

Your project. Your decision.

Live

  1. Smith receives your request and hands it to the router. You do: Type or say: add a dark-mode toggle to settings.

  2. A code change becomes a mission, rather than a direct answer. You do: Check that the request says what you want.

  3. The planner divides the mission into briefs for named agents. You do: Answer if the planner needs a decision.

  4. The agent changes a private git worktree, not your project. You do: Watch progress; your working files stay untouched.

  5. A blocked mission parks with a question, then resumes. You do: Answer the question from your open device.

  6. The finished mission stops with its diff ready to read. You do: Read the changed lines and the review.

  7. APPLY merges the branch. DISCARD removes the private copy. You do: Choose APPLY or DISCARD. Nothing merges for you.

AGENTS + SKILLS

A team you can read and extend.

Live

  1. Hears you, answers aloud and on screen, and routes requests. You: Press TALK or type; review any action it proposes.

  2. Designs one ready-to-save agent file from your description. You: Describe the job, read the persona file, then save it.

  3. Reads Hub facts and proposes a repair in a private copy of the Hub repo. You: Review the checked diff and press APPLY. Restarting is yours.

  4. Finds app controls by name and checks each approved action. You: Read the proposed press and approve or refuse it.

  5. Plans the mission and assigns briefs to worker agents. Writes no code. You: Answer its question if it asks one. Otherwise nothing.

  6. Ships the smallest change that works, in the project's own style. Leaves what you didn't ask about alone. You: Nothing. You read the result in the diff.

  7. The skeptic. Never edits; says ship, changes-needed or blocked, and points at the line. You: Read its verdict, then the diff.

  8. Tries to break it before you do: writes the tests, runs them, reports what failed and why. You: Nothing. The failures show up in the review.

YOU DO

  2. The skill is made available to your agents. You do: Press ADD TO MY AGENTS.

  3. A SKILL.md file describes how to do the work. You do: Write the instructions, then add the skill.
